Research & publications

Current projects and papers

Efficient diffusion language models

Phase3Lock: Three-Phase Locking for Efficient Diffusion Language Models

In progress, Sep 2026 to present, with Prof. Zhiqiang Shen and team. Targeting ICML 2027.

Separates token commitment, representation reuse, and selective computation, with distinct criteria for commitment risk, stale-reference risk, and runtime efficiency. Existing acceleration methods conflate at least two of these decisions; governing each independently matches full-compute answer quality while improving inference efficiency.

Reasoning model evaluation

LLM Reasoning Injection and Interruptible Reasoning: Selective Update Acceptance

In progress, Aug 2026 to present, with Prof. Zangir Iklassov.

Developing, to our knowledge, the first benchmark jointly testing whether reasoning models accept valid, authorized mid-inference updates while rejecting false claims and adversarial instructions. A factorized protocol separates update engagement from answer correctness, and a linear-probing study relates hidden-state representations of acceptance to model behavior. Inspired by “Are Large Reasoning Models Interruptible?”

Multilingual and cultural evaluation

EoKono: Benchmarking LLM's Understanding of Esperanto Language and Culture

Coauthor. Under review at ACL Rolling Review.

An Esperanto benchmark of 23 language models and 2,667 questions reviewed by nine qualified speakers. Compares multiple-choice, true/false, and short-answer evaluation, revealing gaps between linguistic knowledge and understanding of Esperanto history and culture.

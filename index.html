Selected work

Phase3Lock: Three-Phase Locking for Efficient Diffusion Language Models

In progress · With Prof. Zhiqiang Shen and team · Targeting ICML 2027

Speeds up diffusion language models by deciding separately when to commit to a token, when to reuse earlier representations, and when to compute, matching full-compute answer quality while improving efficiency.

LLM Reasoning Injection and Interruptible Reasoning: Selective Update Acceptance

In progress · With Prof. Zangir Iklassov

Tests whether reasoning models accept valid, authorized updates mid-inference while rejecting false claims and adversarial instructions. To our knowledge, the first benchmark to test both together.

Metric Choice Determines Semantic Geometry in LLM Hidden States

First author · ICML 2026 Mechanistic Interpretability Workshop

Raw L2 distance tracks norm growth across layers, while centered cosine reveals semantic, topic, and category structure.

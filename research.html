In progress

Phase3Lock: Three-Phase Locking for Efficient Diffusion Language Models

With Prof. Zhiqiang Shen and team · Sep 2026 – present · Targeting ICML 2027

Makes diffusion language models faster without giving up answer quality.

  • Introduced a three-way separation of token commitment, representation reuse, and selective computation, with distinct criteria for commitment risk, stale-reference risk, and runtime efficiency.
  • Systematically evaluated major prior acceleration strategies, showing that existing methods conflate at least two of these decisions and obscure the source of quality degradation.
  • Developed a three-phase locking strategy that governs each decision independently, matching full-compute answer quality while improving inference efficiency and outperforming prior methods that trade quality for speed.

LLM Reasoning Injection and Interruptible Reasoning: Selective Update Acceptance

With Prof. Zangir Iklassov · Aug 2026 – present · Inspired by “Are Large Reasoning Models Interruptible?”

Do reasoning models accept a valid, authorized update mid-inference, while rejecting false claims and adversarial instructions?

  • Developing, to our knowledge, the first benchmark jointly testing whether reasoning models accept valid, authorized mid-inference updates while rejecting false claims and adversarial instructions.
  • Designed a factorized evaluation protocol separating update engagement from final-answer correctness, with a linear-probing study relating hidden-state representations of acceptance to model behavior.

Learned Depth of Field

With Prof. Lingqi Yan · Joint project · Sep 2026 – present

Depth of field is the soft blur behind a sharp subject. This project explores whether a neural network can produce it at interactive speed, with quality close to traditional rendering methods.

A joint project: we build the rendering testbed, generate training data, train neural models, and evaluate them, and we are now extending the work to video.

EoKono: Benchmarking LLM's Understanding of Esperanto Language and Culture

Coauthor · ACL Rolling Review

How well do language models understand Esperanto language and culture?

  • Coauthored an Esperanto benchmark: 23 language models, 2,667 questions reviewed by nine qualified speakers.
  • Compared multiple-choice, independent true/false, and short-answer evaluation, revealing gaps between linguistic knowledge and understanding of Esperanto history and culture.
  • Examined how prompt language affects cultural knowledge and geographical representation in generated stories.
